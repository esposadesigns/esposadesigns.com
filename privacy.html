Privacy Policy

Clear terms for working with Esposa Designs.

Last updated May 2026

Esposa Designs respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you visit our website, contact us, or use our services.

Information We Collect

We may collect information you provide directly, including your name, email address, business name, property details, brand materials, and any other information you choose to share with us.

  • Contact information when you email or inquire about services
  • Property information needed to create or maintain your guest hub
  • Basic website usage information, such as pages visited or general traffic data

How We Use Information

We use the information we collect to respond to inquiries, provide our services, create and maintain digital guest hubs, improve our website, and communicate with clients about their projects.

Sharing of Information

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We may share limited information with trusted service providers only when needed to operate our website, deliver services, process communications, or maintain client projects.

Client Materials

If you provide property photos, logos, brand assets, copy, links, recommendations, or other materials, we use those materials only to create, maintain, or discuss your guest hub and related services.

Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the information we receive. However, no online transmission or storage method is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your Choices

You may contact us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information we hold about you, subject to any information we need to retain for business, legal, or operational reasons.

Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy can be sent to hello@esposadesigns.com.
